Privacy Policy

Quran Whisperer for Android · Last updated 17 November 2026

Quran Whisperer has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your recordings are processed on your phone and are never uploaded or stored. The developer does not collect any of your data.

Microphone

The app asks for microphone access so it can hear a Quran recitation and work out which surah and ayah is being recited. The audio is turned into text by a speech model that runs on your phone. Audio is kept in memory only while it is being processed, is never saved to storage, and never leaves the device. This includes auto search, which listens in the background (with a visible notification) only when you turn it on, and stops when you turn it off.

What is stored on your phone

  • Your settings (such as text size, translation and voice).
  • The auto search history: which surahs were recognised and when. You can clear it at any time in the app.
  • Downloaded files: the speech model, translations, and any recorded English voice you choose to save for offline use.

All of this stays on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Network requests

The app uses the internet only to download content. It never sends your audio, your history or your settings. The services below receive ordinary web requests, so they can see your IP address and which file was asked for:

  • Hugging Face (huggingface.co): the speech model, downloaded once on first use.
  • Al Quran Cloud (api.alquran.cloud) and Quran.com (api.quran.com): translations other than English, the first time you choose one.
  • Islamic Network CDN (cdn.islamic.network): the recorded English voice, when it is played or saved for offline use.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy.

Reading aloud

When the app reads a translation aloud with your device's voice, it passes the translation text to Android's text-to-speech engine. Most engines run on the device; if you have chosen an online voice, your text-to-speech provider may process that text under its own policy. Your audio is never passed to it.

Permissions

  • Microphone: to recognise the recitation.
  • Notifications: for auto search's ongoing notification and the surahs it finds.
  • Foreground service and keep awake: so auto search can keep listening while the screen is off.
  • Internet: for the downloads listed above.

Sharing and selling data

No personal data is collected, so none is shared with or sold to anyone.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on the project's GitHub issues page.
